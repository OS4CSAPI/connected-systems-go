## Asymmetry the issue describes — confirmed

T1 vs. T2: same semantic intent (optional field has no value) → different
HTTP outcomes (201 vs. 400). The issue body's reproduction is verbatim
correct.

## Adjacent diagnostic finding — required-field null vs. omission

Compare T4 vs. T5:

- T4 (omit required): `"... is required by datastream schema"` — clear
  diagnostic; tells the client to add the missing field.
- T5 (required=null): `"... must be a number"` — tells the client the
  type is wrong, but conceals that the value-shape (`null`) is the
  precise problem.

This is a pre-existing diagnostic-clarity wart that's adjacent to but
distinct from the optional-null asymmetry. The diagnostic confusion is
real even if the outcome (400) is correct.
